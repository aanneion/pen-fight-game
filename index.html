DESK DUEL   /   A tiny escape from your tabs.Made for the “one more round” people.

Recess rules.

The goal: knock the other pen off the desk without losing yours. A pen is out when its center crosses the desk edge.

Your move: grab anywhere on your pen, pull away from where you want it to go, then release. The dotted arrow shows your shot. Hit off-center for a little spin.

Take turns after both pens stop. Both fall? It’s a draw. Rounds are unlimited — keep your own friendly rivalry going.

Keyboard: focus the desk. ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, Space flick. Esc cancels a drag.

Your desk, your rules.

Easy is a little unpredictable. Hard aims more precisely and puts more power behind each shot.

Changes apply to the next shot. No accounts, ads, or tracking. Just play.
